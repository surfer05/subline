the markers

Hollow filling in is a shape change, not a colour change, so the upgrade is legible in greyscale. Hovering either one names it in words.

  • ○
    Quick — arrives within a second. Hollow: provisional, may still change.
  • ●
    Careful — replaces the quick line in place. Filled: settled.
  • nl?
    Unsure of the language. The translation is still shown; the doubt is about where it came from.
