Installing

The security warning

On a Mac, Subline is signed and checked by Apple, so macOS only asks once if you want to open it. The Windows build isn't code-signed yet. I haven't paid for a certificate, so Windows shows a warning the first time. It's the unknown-developer warning, not a malware detection. Here are the steps.

On a Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Subline to Applications.

    If macOS later says Subline is not supported on this Mac, you have an Intel Mac. Download the Intel version instead.

  2. Open Subline from Applications.

    macOS asks once"Subline" is an app downloaded from the internet. Are you sure you want to open it?

    Click Open.

  3. Follow Subline's screens.

    Click Continue. If Discord is open, click Quit Discord for me. Pick the language you read.

  4. Paste your code, or skip it.

    Bought a plan? Paste the code from the Dodo Payments email and click Save code. Check spam. No code? Click Continue without a code. Your first 7 days are still free and automatic.

  5. Turn on Subline.

    Click Continue. System Settings opens. Turn Subline on under App Management. If macOS asks to quit Subline, choose Later. Subline waits for you and carries on by itself. Closed that window? Click Open it again.

  6. Discord opens with Subline.

    Messages in other languages get a translation under them.

On Windows

  1. Keep the download.

    Your browser may saySubline-Setup isn't commonly downloaded.

    In Edge, click ... next to the file, then Keep. Then click Show more, then Keep anyway. In Chrome, click Keep.

  2. Open the Subline-Setup file you downloaded.

    Windows saysWindows protected your PC.

    Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Click Install, then Finish.

    Leave Run Subline ticked. Subline opens.

  4. Follow Subline's screens.

    Click Continue. If Discord is open, click Quit Discord for me. Pick the language you read.

  5. Paste your code, or skip it.

    Bought a plan? Paste the code from the Dodo Payments email and click Save code. Check spam. No code? Click Continue without a code. Your first 7 days are still free and automatic. Subline then opens Discord.
