Installing

The security warning

The builds aren't code-signed. I haven't paid for a certificate, so your computer shows a warning the first time you open the app. It's the unknown-developer warning, not a malware detection. Here are the steps.

  1. Drag Subline to Applications.

    From the .dmg you downloaded.

  2. Right-click Subline → Open.

    macOS saysApple could not verify "Subline" is free of malware…

    Use right-click, not double-click. Right-click gives you an Open button.

  3. If that doesn't work: Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

    Confirm with your password. You only do this once.

  1. Run Subline-Setup.exe.

    Windows saysWindows protected your PC.

    Click More info, then Run anyway.

  2. Click Install.

    It closes Discord, patches it, and reopens it.
