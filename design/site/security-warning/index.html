Installing

About that warning

The builds aren't code-signed yet — I haven't paid for a certificate. So your computer warns you once. It means "unverified," not "unsafe." Here's the click-through.

  1. Drag Subline to Applications.

    From the .dmg you downloaded.

  2. Right-click Subline → Open.

    macOS saysApple could not verify "Subline" is free of malware…

    Right-click, not double-click. That gives you an Open button the double-click doesn't.

  3. Still stuck? Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

    Confirm with your password. Once, for this app, done.

  1. Run Subline-Setup.exe.

    Windows saysWindows protected your PC.

    More info → Run anyway. That's the whole trick.

  2. Click Install.

    It closes Discord, patches it, reopens it. You're done.
