Privacy

Where your messages go

Translation cannot happen without sending the text somewhere. Here is every place it goes, in plain terms. We would rather you decide against Subline knowing this than install it without.

WhatGoes whereKept?
Message textGoogle Translate for a Quick translation. Anthropic's API for a Careful one, if you've added a key.Not by us. We store nothing and run no server.
Who sent itNowhere. Names, avatars, server and channel names are never sent.—
Your API keyStays on your computer, in Discord's own settings storage. Sent only to Anthropic, only by you.On your machine.
Usage dataNone collected. No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry of any kind.—
  • off
    Direct messages are not translated unless you turn them on. Off by default, per conversation, and it stays off until you say otherwise.
  • off
    Messages already in your reading language are never sent anywhere. Detection happens on your computer first.
  • you
    You can switch translation off per channel. Nothing leaves your machine from a channel you've disabled.

The honest part

Google and Anthropic have their own terms, and once text reaches them it is governed by those, not by us. If a conversation is sensitive enough that a third party reading it would matter, turn Subline off for that channel — or don't install it. That is a real trade, and it is yours to make.
