Step one of four

Read what your friends are actually saying.

Subline puts a translation underneath any message written in a language you don't read — inside the Discord you already use. Same app, same account, same servers.

  • 03

Step two of four

Two speeds of translation

Quick arrives almost immediately. Careful replaces it a few seconds later, if you set up a key. You can do that now, or any time later.

  • ○
    Quick · free, no account

    Google Translate. Good enough to follow a conversation. This is on from the moment you finish.

  • ●
    Careful · free key, two minutes to set up

    An AI model that reads the rest of the conversation, so slang, mixed languages and jokes survive. It quietly replaces the quick line when it's ready.

Step four of four

macOS needs your permission

Discord is an app, and changing an app needs your say-so. macOS calls that App Management. The next screen is the system's, not ours.

  • →
    What we do with it

    Add one translation file inside Discord. That's the whole of it.

  • ←
    How you take it back

    System Settings › Privacy & Security › App Management. Turn Subline off, any time.

macOS will show its own dialog when you continue. Choosing Open System Settings there is the one that works.
