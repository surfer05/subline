## Subline 0.2.0

Subline is now paid. There is no free plan and no trial.

- **Automatic, $4.99 once.** ≈ under every message, profile and embed. Codes decoded (Morse, binary and more). 5 ✦ previews a day.
- **AI, $1.99 a month or $19.99 a year.** ✦ on everything. Needs Automatic.
- **Server codes.** A code from your server gives you Automatic.
- **3 computers.** A code works on up to 3 computers. A computer unused for 30 days frees its place. AI works on the computers where you bought it or entered its code.

### Who gets Automatic at no charge

Early users get Automatic at no charge: anyone whose Subline used ✦ AI at least once before September 29, 2026, and every AI subscriber active at release.

Nothing to do. Early users see "Thanks for being early. Automatic is yours, free." when 0.2.0 starts. For AI subscribers, Automatic stays if the subscription ends later.

Everyone else sees an Activate button in Discord, and a new install asks in the installer.

### Installing

Download the installer for your computer below. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn that the app is unrecognised: choose More info, then Run anyway.
