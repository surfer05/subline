## Subline 0.2.2

- **Uninstall works again after a Discord update.** On Windows, a Discord update could make Subline think another mod was installed. Uninstall then refused, and Subline stopped repairing itself. Fixed.
- **Subline repairs itself after every Discord update,** even while Discord is open.

If Uninstall failed for you: download the new installer from the site and open it. It installs over the old one. Then Uninstall works.
